### 🚀 About GrowGix

**GrowGix** is an engineering and digital product innovation studio headquartered in Odisha, India, collaborating with ambitious brands and enterprises globally. We build robust digital ecosystems: from high-throughput distributed CRM engines and custom cloud software, to ultra-responsive web & mobile applications and autonomous AI-driven automation workflows.

> *Turning business vision into performant, scalable, and intelligent software.*
